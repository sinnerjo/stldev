Pitch Invasion

The stands have emptied onto the field. You have one ball. Kick it at the invaders and it comes back to your feet, so every shot needs good timing. Hold the kick to charge a power shot that cuts through a whole line.

Who is coming

  • OppositionFast. One touch sends them off.
  • KeepersBig, tough, and they dive at you.
  • RefereesKeep their distance and show cards. Yellow hurts. Red hurts a lot more.
  • Rowdy fansSlow and loud. The shouting hurts.
  • CoachesHang back and fire up everyone nearby. Take them out first.
